# Elevate ERP — Public Landing Page Brief

Use this document as the **content & structure source of truth** when designing or rebuilding the public marketing landing page (`/`).

It describes what the product **is**, what it **includes today**, and how to present that clearly to a first-time visitor (Kenya / East Africa SME and mid-market operators).

---

## 1. Product one-liner

**Elevate** is an all-in-one business ERP for teams that sell, stock, deliver, collect money, pay staff, and manage people — in one workspace.

Not “HR only.” Not “accounting only.”  
It connects **operations → customers → inventory → money → people**.

---

## 2. Positioning (what to say on the page)

### Problem
Growing companies juggle spreadsheets, WhatsApp, and disconnected tools for:

- quotations and invoices  
- stock and dispatch  
- customer debts and payments  
- expenses and M-Pesa  
- payroll and statutory deductions  
- attendance, leave, and performance  

### Promise
One branded workspace where:

1. Sales creates quotes and invoices  
2. Warehouse fulfills and dispatches  
3. Accounts tracks cash in/out and debtors  
4. HR/payroll pays people and tracks performance  
5. Managers see what is happening in real time  

### Audience
- Company admins / owners  
- Finance / accounts teams  
- Sales and field teams  
- Warehouse / inventory managers  
- HR and line managers  
- Employees (self-service portal)

### Tone
- Clear, operational, trustworthy  
- Kenya-aware (M-Pesa, KES, eTIMS, NSSF, SHA, PAYE)  
- Avoid hype and generic “AI workforce” language unless paired with real modules

## 3. Recommended landing page structure

Build the page as **one scrollable story**. Keep the first viewport simple.

| Section | Purpose | What to put |
|--------|---------|-------------|
| **1. Nav** | Orientation | Logo **Elevate**, links: Product, Modules, How it works, Pricing (if any), Login, **Get started** |
| **2. Hero** | Brand + promise | Brand name large; one headline; one supporting sentence; primary CTA **Start free / Get started**; secondary CTA **See modules** |
| **3. Trust strip** | Credibility | Short line: multi-tenant companies · role-based access · Kenya tax & M-Pesa ready |
| **4. What Elevate is** | Definition | 3–4 sentences: ERP for sell–stock–cash–people |
| **5. Module grid** | Feature proof | Cards for each major product area (below) |
| **6. Money flow story** | Differentiator | Diagram/copy: Invoice paid → Cashbook · Expense paid → Cashbook · Salary marked paid → Cashbook · Reconcile later |
| **7. Roles** | Who it’s for | Admin · Sales · Accounts · Warehouse · Employee |
| **8. How it works** | Onboarding | 4 steps: Create company → Enable modules → Invite team → Run day-to-day |
| **9. Deep features** | Detail | Optional accordions: Inventory, Accounts, HR, Clients/Field, Fleet |
| **10. CTA** | Conversion | Repeat get-started + login |
| **11. Footer** | Legal/contact | Product links, support, © Elevate |

- **Eyebrow:** Business ERP for growing teams  
- **Headline:** Run sales, stock, cash, and people in one Elevate workspace  
- **Supporting:** From quotation to invoice, dispatch to payment, expense to payroll — track the work and the money without switching tools.  
- **Primary CTA:** Get started  
- **Secondary CTA:** Explore modules  

### Alternate headline options

1. One ERP for operations, money, and your team  
2. Sell, stock, collect, and pay — without the spreadsheet chaos  
3. Your company OS: inventory, accounts, and people performance

## 4. What the ERP has (module catalogue)

Use these as **landing cards**. Keep titles short; put detail in subtext.

### A. Core & company setup
- Company workspace (multi-tenant)  
- Users, roles, and page access by department  
- Branches / departments  
- Branding (logo, colours) on documents and admin UI  
- System settings, stamps, invoice document settings  

### B. Sales & stock (Inventory Manager)
- Products, categories, manufacturers  
- Stock entries / inventory levels  
- Quotations → approval → convert to invoice  
- Sales invoices, delivery notes, PDF documents  
- Credit notes  
- Dispatch / fulfillment workflows  
- Stock checks and history  
- Analytics (product, trends, outsourced)  
- Services jobs (where enabled)  
- Importation / sourcing module  

### C. Clients & field
- Client directory and client groups  
- Installed machines / after-sales context  
- Telesales and sales activity  
- Complaints  
- Bulk SMS campaigns  
- Field management reports & performance  
- Sales reports  

### D. Accounts & finance
Landing inside the product: `/admin/accounts`

| Module | What visitors should understand |
|--------|----------------------------------|
| **Expenses** | Record company spend, categories, claims, export |
| **Sales & Receivables** | Payments, debtors, aging, statements, credit/debit notes |
| **Cashflow & Banking** | Cash, bank, M-Pesa balances, cashbook, transfers, reconciliation |
| **Payroll & Statutory** | Payslips, net pay, SHA/PAYE/NSSF/HELB style reports |
| **Inventory Accounting** | Valuation / management financial analytics |
| **Purchases & Payables** | Supplier bills & creditors (roadmap / expanding) |

**Money connection (important differentiator to show on the landing):**

- Customer pays an invoice → recorded in cashbook (in)  
- Company pays an expense → recorded in cashbook (out)  
- Salary marked paid → recorded in cashbook (out)  
- Later: tick lines against bank/M-Pesa statements (**reconcile**)  

Also: **eTIMS / KRA OSCU** posting for electronic tax invoices (Kenya).

### E. People & HR
- Attendance  
- Leave requests & approvals  
- Payroll generation & employee payslips  
- Meetings (incl. video/WebRTC where enabled)  
- Contracts, alerts, suggestions  
- Badges / awards, polls  
- Resource bookings & allocations  

### F. Performance
- KPI configuration  
- Performance reviews / PDPs  
- 360° feedback pools & surveys  
- Reports hub  

### G. Recruitment
- Job postings  
- Application forms / careers pages  
- Applications inbox  
- Job analytics  
- Communications  

### H. Fleet (where enabled)
- Vehicles register  
- Fleet tracking / operations  

### I. Portals by role
- **Admin** — full company control  
- **Manager** — team approvals, messages, performance  
- **Sales** — clients, quotes, field activity  
- **Employee** — attendance, leave, payslips, tasks, profile

## 5. Feature highlights worth calling out (proof points)

Use as bullets under “Built for how Kenyan businesses actually operate”:

1. **Document PDFs** — branded invoices, quotations, debit/credit notes  
2. **M-Pesa** — expense prompts / receipts; cashbook accounts for tills & paybills  
3. **eTIMS readiness** — post tax invoices where configured  
4. **Debtors & aging** — see who owes what, and for how long  
5. **Cashbook truth** — one place for inflows/outflows across cash, bank, M-Pesa  
6. **Role-based modules** — enable only what each company needs  
7. **Employee self-service** — less admin chasing for leave, payslips, attendance

## 7. How it works (4 steps for the landing)

1. **Create your company** — sign up and set branding, branches, and departments.  
2. **Turn on modules** — enable Inventory, Accounts, HR, Clients, Fleet as needed.  
3. **Invite your team** — admins, sales, warehouse, finance, employees get the right access.  
4. **Run the business daily** — quote → invoice → collect → expense → pay salaries → reconcile.

## 8. Visual direction (for designers / implementers)

- Match product reality: **operational ERP**, not purple SaaS cliché.  
- Show real UI screenshots if available: invoice PDF, cashbook, receivables hub, stock invoices list.  
- Prefer one strong hero composition: brand + headline + CTA + one product screenshot.  
- Avoid stuffing the first viewport with stats grids and feature chips.  
- Use company primary colour from branding when demoing tenant UI; for marketing site pick a single clear brand colour system.

## 10. What NOT to claim (accuracy)

Do not claim as fully complete unless built:

- Full double-entry general ledger / trial balance as finished product  
- Complete purchases & payables suite as finished (mark as growing / coming soon if shown)  
- “Fully automated bank feed import” unless implemented  

Do claim what exists:

- Operational inventory + sales documents  
- Receivables, expenses, cashbook, reconciliation workflow  
- Payroll + statutory reporting  
- HR / performance / recruitment suites  
- Kenya-oriented payments & tax hooks (M-Pesa, eTIMS)
